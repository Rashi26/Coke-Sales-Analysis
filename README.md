# Coke-Sales-Analysis🥤

**Excel Dashboard | Sales Analytics | Business Intelligence**

An Excel-based sales analysis project exploring **Coca-Cola's retail performance across the United States**.

The project transforms transaction-level sales data into interactive dashboards, PivotTables, and visual analyses to understand **sales, volume, pricing, profitability, retailer performance, regional performance, and seasonality**.

---

## 🎯 Business Question

> **How can Coca-Cola optimize sales and profitability across U.S. retailers and regions?**

The analysis looks beyond total revenue to understand the relationship between **sales volume, pricing, operating profit, and margin**.

---

## 📊 What I Analyzed

* 🏪 **Retailer performance** — sales, units, profit & margin
* 🗺️ **Regional performance** — sales and profitability across U.S. regions
* 🥤 **Brand performance** — revenue and volume by beverage
* 📅 **Sales trends** — monthly and seasonal patterns
* 💰 **Pricing & volume** — relationship between price and units sold
* 📈 **Profitability** — operating profit and operating margin

---

## 💡 Highlights

| Metric                   |     Result |
| ------------------------ | ---------: |
| Total Sales              | **$8.68M** |
| Units Sold               | **17.15M** |
| Operating Profit         | **$3.17M** |
| Overall Operating Margin | **~36.5%** |

Some of the key findings:

* **Sodapop** is the largest retailer by sales.
* **BevCo** has the highest operating margin.
* The **West** is the largest region by sales and total profit.
* The **South** has the strongest operating margin.
* **Coca-Cola** leads the portfolio in sales and unit volume.
* Sales show strong seasonal peaks during **summer and December**.

## 🚀 Key Takeaway

The analysis shows that **scale and efficiency are not always the same thing**.

The largest retailer and region drive substantial revenue, while smaller segments can deliver stronger margins. Understanding these differences helps identify where Coca-Cola can focus on **growth, operational efficiency, pricing, and profitability**.
